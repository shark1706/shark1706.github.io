Privacy Policy - Personal Assistant

This application is used only by its owner (shaked.mail@gmail.com) on his own computer.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the app reads the owner's Gmail messages, Google Calendar events and Google Contacts, and creates or changes calendar events at the owner's request.

How data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's questions, send him reminders and a daily summary. To produce answers, relevant text is sent to the Anthropic Claude API for processing. Data is not sold, shared with other parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and reminders are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
